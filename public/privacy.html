Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 11, 2026

The short version: Octopus is self-hosted. Your trading data, broker keys, strategies, and results live on your own machine — not on our servers. We have no accounts, take no payments, and cannot see your trading. This website itself collects essentially nothing about you.

1. Who We Are

This policy covers theoctopusapp.com (the "Site") and the open-source Octopus software (the "Software"). Questions: contact@theoctopusapp.com.

2. The Software Runs on Your Machine

Octopus is downloaded and run by you, on your own computer or server. As a result:

  • Your broker API keys are encrypted at rest on your machine (Fernet/AES) and are never transmitted to us.
  • Your strategies, signals, trades, paper-sim results, and logs are stored locally (under ~/.octopus/). We have no access to them.
  • We do not operate a server that runs your trading, so we cannot and do not collect your trading activity, balances, or positions.

If you enable the optional LLM features, the Software sends compact market context to the AI provider you configure (e.g. OpenRouter) using your own API key, under that provider's privacy policy. It never sends your broker credentials to the model.

3. What This Website Collects

The Site is a static page hosted on Cloudflare Pages. We run no ad trackers and sell no data. Cloudflare may process standard, aggregated request logs (IP, user agent, timestamp) for security and basic analytics, per Cloudflare's privacy policy. We do not set advertising cookies.

4. If You Contact Us

If you email us or open a GitHub issue, we receive what you choose to send (e.g. your email address and message) and use it only to respond. GitHub issues are public — please don't include secrets.

5. No Accounts, No Payments

The Software is free and self-hosted. We do not create user accounts, process payments, or store payment information. There is no subscription and no billing.

6. Your Data, Your Control

Because your trading data stays on your device, you control it directly — delete ~/.octopus/ at any time to remove it. For any limited information we hold (e.g. an email you sent us), you may request access or deletion at contact@theoctopusapp.com.

7. Children's Privacy

The Software and Site are not directed to anyone under 18, and trading is not suitable for minors.

8. Changes & Contact

We may update this policy; the "last updated" date reflects the current version. Reach us at contact@theoctopusapp.com or github.com/Code-weaver1/octopus.
