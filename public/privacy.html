Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 13, 2026

Summary: Octopus collects only what it needs to operate your businesses autonomously. We store your account credentials encrypted, never sell your data, and you can delete everything at any time.

1. Who We Are

Octopus ("we", "us", "our") is an autonomous AI business operating platform provided by Octopus Technologies. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our platform, website, desktop application, and API (collectively, the "Service").

For questions about this policy, contact us at privacy@theoctopusapp.com.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you register, we collect:

  • Email address — for authentication, verification, and communication
  • Name — for display and personalization
  • Password — stored as a bcrypt hash (we never store your plaintext password)
  • Country, city, timezone — for proxy geo-matching and cycle scheduling

2.2 Platform Credentials

When you connect external platforms (Twitter, Instagram, Facebook, MQL5, LinkedIn, TikTok), we collect:

  • Session cookies — encrypted at rest using Fernet symmetric encryption
  • Platform username — for display and session management
  • Browser fingerprint data — screen dimensions, user agent, timezone (used to replicate your browser profile for automation)

We never ask for or store your platform passwords. We only store session cookies that you explicitly provide through the desktop application's cookie capture flow.

2.3 Usage Data

We automatically collect:

  • Actions performed by the autonomous agent on your behalf (tool executions, posts made, engagement actions)
  • Revenue, conversion funnel events, and campaign performance metrics
  • Chat messages between you and the AI assistant
  • IP address and request metadata (for rate limiting and security)

2.4 Payment Information

We process payments through third-party providers (Pesapal, Flutterwave, PayPal). We store:

  • Transaction references, amounts, status, and payment method type
  • Provider-specific transaction IDs for reconciliation

We do not store credit card numbers, mobile money PINs, or bank account details. These are handled entirely by the payment provider.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate the Service — executing autonomous business actions on your connected platforms using your session credentials
  • To improve AI decisions — the AI brain (Riley) uses your goals, metrics, and performance history to decide what actions to take each cycle
  • To manage subscriptions — processing payments, enforcing tier limits, and sending billing-related communications
  • To communicate with you — email verification, password resets, system notifications, and optional WhatsApp reports
  • To ensure security — rate limiting, detecting abuse, and protecting platform integrity

4. Autonomous Actions on Your Behalf

Octopus performs automated actions on external platforms using credentials you provide. This includes posting content, engaging with other users (likes, comments, follows), checking seller dashboards, replying to messages, and scanning the web for business opportunities. These actions are taken on your behalf and under your authorization.

You can:

  • Disconnect any platform at any time to stop all automated actions on it
  • Set goals and constraints that guide what the AI chooses to do
  • Review all actions taken via the activity log in your dashboard
  • Enable safe mode to preview actions before they execute

5. Data Storage and Security

  • Encryption at rest: Platform cookies are encrypted with Fernet (AES-128-CBC). Payment provider API keys are encrypted with a separate key.
  • Password hashing: bcrypt with a cost factor of 12.
  • JWT authentication: Tokens are signed with HMAC-SHA256, expire in 24 hours, and can be revoked on logout.
  • Tenant isolation: Each user's data is stored in isolated directories with per-user database scoping.
  • Network security: Per-user residential proxy assignments ensure your automation traffic does not originate from a shared IP pool.
  • File permissions: Credential files and database files are restricted to owner-only access (chmod 600/700).

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data.

We share data only in these limited circumstances:

  • Payment providers (Pesapal, Flutterwave, PayPal) — your email, name, and transaction amount to process payments
  • External platforms (Twitter, Instagram, etc.) — your session cookies are sent to these platforms to execute actions you authorized
  • AI processing — your business context is processed by a locally-hosted AI model (Ollama). No data is sent to third-party AI services for autonomous cycle decisions.
  • Legal obligations — if required by law, court order, or to protect our legal rights

7. Data Retention

  • Account data: Retained while your account is active. Deleted within 30 days of account deletion request.
  • Activity logs: Retained for 90 days, then pruned automatically.
  • Funnel/revenue data: Retained for 90 days (rolling).
  • Chat messages: Retained while the session exists. You can delete sessions at any time.
  • Payment records: Retained for 7 years for legal and tax compliance.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Correct — update your account information through the Settings page
  • Delete — request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Disconnect — remove any connected platform at any time, which immediately deletes stored cookies and stops automation
  • Export — download your revenue, campaign, and activity data
  • Restrict — pause automation on any or all platforms without deleting your account

To exercise these rights, email privacy@theoctopusapp.com or use the relevant controls in your dashboard.

9. Cookies and Tracking

The Octopus website uses no third-party tracking cookies, analytics services, or advertising pixels. We use only:

  • A JWT token stored in localStorage for authentication in the desktop app
  • Your server URL preference stored in localStorage

10. Children's Privacy

Octopus is not intended for use by anyone under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. International Data

Octopus is operated from Uganda. If you use the Service from outside Uganda, your information may be transferred to and processed in Uganda or other countries where our servers are located. By using the Service, you consent to this transfer.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by email or through a notification in the platform. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
